# MW-02 - Health Assessment - ONE PR

**PR title:** `fix(flow-1): add optional member health assessment`
**Depends on:** MW-01.

## Scope
- Implement a *versioned optional questionnaire* under Member Profile (dedicated Health & Training section) with these fixed yes/no/prefer-not-to-answer questions: (1) injury or mobility issue affecting exercise; (2) professional advice to restrict strenuous activity; (3) dizziness/chest discomfort during exertion; (4) health concern the Coach should know; (5) wants consultation before first class. Safe wording and optional completion; no invasive open-text medical history required.
- Persist responses securely, allow edit and consent withdrawal, with timestamps/version. Compute only advisory `NO_REPORTED_CONCERNS`, `COACH_DISCUSSION_RECOMMENDED`, or `INCOMPLETE`. `NO_REPORTED_CONCERNS` does not mean medically cleared.
- Show privacy purpose, limited Coach sharing with Member permission. Currently assigned Coach may see only minimal Coach-safety-summary for actual associated class/Member; unrelated Coach and Receptionist cannot read raw responses. No health answers in general notifications, logs or screenshots.
- If class age checks exist, rely on DOB from MW-01, not arbitrary questionnaire 'diagnosis' to block Booking.

## Acceptance
1. Member may skip and later update; invalid values denied; edit persists after refresh.
2. Advisory only - no invented health diagnosis or automatic unsafe-to-train clearance.
3. Member/assigned Coach permission enforcement is tested; unrelated staff cannot fetch health detail.
4. All screenshots use fabricated safe test answers; copy and access controls are visible.

## Evidence
- Screenshot A: Member Health & Training questionnaire on desktop.
- Screenshot B: saved advisory outcome and privacy statement; optional third assigned Coach safe summary.
- Test preference/skip, version, consent, resource scope and denied access.

## Out of scope
Medical prescriptions, using AI to diagnose, blanket health restrictions or collecting clinical documents.
