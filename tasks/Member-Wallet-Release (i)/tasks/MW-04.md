# MW-04 - QR Top-up / Receptionist Confirmation - ONE PR

**PR title:** `fix(flow-3): implement QR wallet top-up verification`
**Depends on:** MW-03.

## Scope
- Member chooses top-up VND amount and requests a unique QR transfer intent with recipient bank, amount and unique transfer note; render readable VietQR under existing QR conventions; no uncontrolled QR generation from arbitrary bank details.
- QR creation creates `PENDING` *only*. Receptionist sees staff-only pending confirmation queue and validates manual reference/amount/recipient against bank evidence; authorize and transition exactly once to SUCCESS + wallet ledger credit.
- Separate `SIMULATED` demo QR topups with explicit marking from actual receipts; no simulation claim as real banking/revenue/refund or withdrawable cash. Reject mismatches, duplicate external references, stale/rejected intents, replayed confirmation, cross-user/staff access, and bad amount.
- If existing project already has a QR demo policy that quarantines simulated member identities, preserve it. Design synthetic demo identities within existing restrictions; do not bypass provenance protections for convenience.

## Acceptance
1. Pending QR leaves wallet unchanged.
2. Authorized confirmed top-up credits exactly once, including concurrent duplicate confirmation.
3. Member sees pending/confirmed status; staff confirmation tied to actor/time/reference and retained in history.
4. Incorrect amount, reference mismatch or ineligible funding rejected without changing balance; clearly distinguish demo source.
